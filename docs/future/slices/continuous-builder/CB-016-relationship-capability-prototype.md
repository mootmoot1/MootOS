# CB-016 — Communication and Relationship Capability Prototype

**Status:** Designing

## Purpose
Use a real-value vertical to prove identity, connector, memory, follow-up, and optional payment integration patterns.

## Initial scope
Track people/aliases, artist handles, communication history, last interaction, relationship state, requests, next actions, and weekly/two-week follow-up opportunities.

## Payment rule
Square/payment data is optional through a plug-in adapter. Communication value must work without payment integration.

## Safety
Draft outreach first; sending authority is separately gated.

## Acceptance
The system can identify who may need follow-up and prepare a reasoned draft while keeping provider integrations replaceable.
