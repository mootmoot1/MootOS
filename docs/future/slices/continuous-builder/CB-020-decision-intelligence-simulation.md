# CB-020 — Decision Intelligence and Simulation

**Status:** Designing

## Purpose
Give Chief Builder a disciplined way to explain, challenge, rehearse, and learn from consequential decisions.

## Capabilities
Evidence-backed decision records, alternatives, confidence, what-if analysis, counterfactuals, assumption challenge mode, decision rehearsal, scenario simulation, and post-outcome comparison.

## Principle
Simulation is advisory and must surface uncertainty; it is not permission to execute a risky action.

## Acceptance
A significant decision can be replayed later with what was known, predicted scenarios, chosen path, confidence, and actual outcome.
