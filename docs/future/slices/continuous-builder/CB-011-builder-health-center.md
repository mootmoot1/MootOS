# CB-011 — Builder Health Center

**Status:** Designing

## Purpose
Give high-level development visibility without forcing Moot to micromanage agents.

## Show
Active builds, Ready queue, blockers, failing jobs, PRs awaiting review, staging state, specialist health, throughput, recent failures, and confidence.

## Principle
Summary first; details drill down. Health scores must be explainable, not decorative.

## Acceptance
One view can answer: what is building, what is stuck, what needs me, and whether the overall system is healthy.
