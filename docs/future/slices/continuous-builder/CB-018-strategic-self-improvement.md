# CB-018 — Strategic Self-Improvement Engine

**Status:** Designing

## Purpose
Let MootOS identify its own bottlenecks and propose better architecture/process without changing itself silently.

## Detect
Technical debt, repeated failures, missing capabilities, redundant capability, poor performance, maintainability risk, architecture drift, and opportunities.

## Evaluate
Architecture impact, regression risk, opportunity cost, roadmap alignment, long-term vision alignment, expected ROI, confidence, and research needed.

## Output
Proposal and optional candidate slices; no autonomous major architecture mutation.

## Acceptance
Self-improvement recommendations are evidence-backed, comparable to alternatives, and human-reviewable.
