# CB-009 — Change Proposal System

**Status:** Designing

## Purpose
Allow builders to challenge the roadmap or propose major improvements without silently changing the plan.

## Proposal
Problem/opportunity, evidence, proposed change, benefits, risks, alternatives, effort, roadmap impact, confidence, and recommendation.

## Rule
Reuse of an existing shared component is quiet. Creation of a new shared/platform abstraction or meaningful roadmap interruption requires proposal/approval.

## Acceptance
A major discovered improvement becomes a decision artifact rather than an untracked side quest.
