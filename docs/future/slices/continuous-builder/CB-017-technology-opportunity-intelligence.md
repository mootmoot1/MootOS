# CB-017 — Technology and Opportunity Intelligence

**Status:** Designing

## Purpose
Turn AI/technology developments into useful decisions rather than news spam.

## Briefing format
Approximately top five worthwhile items on a regular cadence. Each item: 60-second summary, why Moot should care, likely MootOS impact, estimated opportunity, recommendation, and read-more reference.

## Ranking
Prefer applicability, reliability, leverage, cost, and roadmap fit over hype.

## Acceptance
The briefing is short enough to scan yet gives enough reasoning to decide what deserves deeper reading or experimentation.
