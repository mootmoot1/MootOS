Why this page does not let you change the model provider yet. Runtime-mutable settings would need their own validated storage, and MootOS deliberately keeps provider/model selection in environment variables for now so a setting can never silently diverge from what is actually deployed. To change the provider or model, update AI_PROVIDER / OPENAI_MODEL in Railway and redeploy.
